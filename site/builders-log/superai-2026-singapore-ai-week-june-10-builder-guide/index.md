# SuperAI 2026 and Singapore AI Week: What Builders Should Know (June 10-14)

> Singapore AI Week kicks off June 8 with SuperAI 2026 as its anchor event on June 10-11. Here's why this event matters for AI builders, who's on stage, and what the neutral-ground framing tells us about where global AI development is heading.


Singapore AI Week runs June 8-14, with **SuperAI 2026** as its anchor event at Marina Bay Sands on June 10-11. If you follow the US-centric AI conference circuit — NeurIPS, GTC, Build, Google I/O — SuperAI is worth knowing about for a reason that goes beyond speaker names: it is explicitly positioned as the place where AI development happens *outside* the US-China axis.

That framing is doing real editorial work in 2026.

## Quick Facts

- **SuperAI dates:** [June 10-11, 2026](https://www.superai.com/sg-ai-week)
- **Location:** [Marina Bay Sands, Singapore](https://www.superai.com/sg-ai-week)
- **Singapore AI Week:** [June 8-14](https://www.superai.com/sg-ai-week)
- **Scale:** [10,000 attendees, 1,500 AI companies, 150+ speakers, 150+ exhibitors, 150+ countries represented](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/superai-singapore-2026-sells-out-asias-largest-ai-event-brings-together-10-000-global-innovators-302795988.html)

## Why Singapore, Why Now

The conference's own framing is "[where the world's AI powers converge on neutral ground](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/superai-2026-returns-to-singapore-as-the-worlds-ai-powers-converge-on-neutral-ground-302725598.html)" — organizers describe Singapore as "one of the few neutral grounds where these groups can meet at scale" as export controls tighten and sovereign AI strategies accelerate globally. That phrase isn't just marketing — it's a reading of the current moment.

Singapore has been positioning itself as a jurisdiction that can work with AI labs across the US-China divide. The government committed [over S$1 billion (2025-2030) to its National AI Research and Development Plan](https://www.mddi.gov.sg/newsroom/singapore-invests-over-s-1-billion-in-national-ai-research-and-development-plan-to-strengthen-ai-research-capabilities-and-our-position-as-global-ai-hub/), and on June 8, 2026 — the same day Singapore AI Week opened — the National Supercomputing Centre [launched its Aspire 2B supercomputer](https://www.mddi.gov.sg/newsroom/opening-address-by-minister-josephine-teo-at-national-supercomputing-centre--nscc--s-launch-of-aspire-2b-supercomputer/), a national compute resource for AI research. The SuperAI speaker roster itself — [Cerebras and San Francisco Compute (US) alongside Z.ai (China)](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/superai-2026-announces-first-speakers-as-singapore-strengthens-role-as-a-neutral-global-ai-hub-302761990.html) — reflects that both-sides courtship. Singapore AI Week is the institutional expression of that bet.

For builders who work in Asia-Pacific markets, or who are building infrastructure that will need to operate under multiple regulatory regimes, the Singapore AI Week speakers and sessions reflect a different axis of concern than US-centric conferences. The tracks on governance, compute access, and fintech AI have a different flavor when the audience includes builders from Southeast Asia, Japan, India, and the Gulf.

## Speaker Highlights for Builders

The most directly relevant speakers for the technical builder audience:

**[Andy Hock (CSO and SVP, Cerebras Systems)](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/superai-2026-announces-first-speakers-as-singapore-strengthens-role-as-a-neutral-global-ai-hub-302761990.html)** — [Cerebras builds wafer-scale AI compute systems](https://www.cerebras.ai/). Hock's session will likely cover the practical tradeoffs between GPU clusters and purpose-built AI silicon for inference workloads — a question that matters if you're running inference at scale and want alternatives to NVIDIA.

**[Evan Conrad (CEO, San Francisco Compute)](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/superai-2026-announces-first-speakers-as-singapore-strengthens-role-as-a-neutral-global-ai-hub-302761990.html)** — [San Francisco Compute](https://sfcompute.com/) is a newer entrant in the GPU access/compute marketplace, founded in 2023. Conrad's session is expected to address the access layer: how builders without enterprise GPU contracts can get compute at scale.

**[Zixuan Li (Head of Z.ai)](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/superai-2026-announces-first-speakers-as-singapore-strengthens-role-as-a-neutral-global-ai-hub-302761990.html)** — [Z.ai (formerly Zhipu AI)](https://z.ai/) is one of the more prominent frontier model developers outside the US-Europe-OpenAI axis. Li's session is expected to touch on frontier scaling, which is increasingly relevant as inference costs drop and the "which model do I use" question expands beyond GPT-4 and Claude.

**Balaji Srinivasan (keynote)** — Srinivasan's keynote is billed as covering ["the personal, private and programmable future of AI"](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/superai-2026-announces-first-speakers-as-singapore-strengthens-role-as-a-neutral-global-ai-hub-302761990.html) — the intersection of personal AI assistants, privacy-preserving inference, and programmable AI agents that builders control rather than platforms. His talks typically function as an essay with a specific thesis; even if you disagree with his conclusions, they're worth reading as a signal of where the decentralized/sovereign AI movement is heading.

**Benedict Evans (annual AI outlook)** — Evans [publishes an annual AI/tech-trends presentation](https://www.ben-evans.com/) that's widely circulated in product and strategy circles, and is [confirmed to present his annual AI outlook and industry trends briefing at SuperAI 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/superai-2026-announces-first-speakers-as-singapore-strengthens-role-as-a-neutral-global-ai-hub-302761990.html). His session will likely cover his current read on where AI development stands, where the hype is overextended, and what the next 12-18 months look like from a market structure perspective.

**[Ramine Tinati (Director, Google DeepMind)](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/superai-2026-announces-first-speakers-as-singapore-strengthens-role-as-a-neutral-global-ai-hub-302761990.html)** — The Google DeepMind presence covers their research and applied AI agenda; expect framing around safety, capabilities, and infrastructure.

## The Six Conference Tracks

SuperAI organizes its program around [six conference pillars](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/superai-2026-returns-to-singapore-as-the-worlds-ai-powers-converge-on-neutral-ground-302725598.html):

1. **Robotics & Embodied AI** — the physical AI layer; humanoid robot deployment, industrial automation
2. **Frontier Models** — model capabilities, alignment research, safety frameworks
3. **AI Infrastructure** — hardware, data centers, energy, supply chain for AI compute
4. **AI in Finance** — AI in financial services, trading, fraud, compliance, payments
5. **BioTech & HealthTech** — AI in drug discovery, diagnostics, clinical workflows
6. **AI's Global Impact** — safety, governance, workforce transformation, and international frameworks

For most builders, the AI Infrastructure and AI's Global Impact tracks will be the most useful if you're watching the recordings. The governance-focused track is particularly interesting given Singapore's regulatory environment and the multi-jurisdictional audience.

## What This Means for Builders Who Aren't Going

If you're not in Singapore this week, the practical value of following SuperAI is:

**Compute alternatives are consolidating.** Cerebras, San Francisco Compute, and the Singapore national compute cluster represent different paths to AI compute that aren't NVIDIA/AWS. If GPU access is a constraint for you, the SuperAI compute track is worth reading for emerging options.

**Asia-Pacific AI development is not a derivative.** The presence of Z.ai, Singapore's sovereign AI infrastructure, and the multi-jurisdictional governance track reflects genuine AI development momentum in markets that US-centric coverage often underweights. If your users are in APAC or your data must stay in-region, this track is worth attention.

**Regulatory divergence is accelerating.** The [EU AI Act](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai), China's AI governance framework, and the patchwork of US state laws are all heading in different directions. The Singapore governance track typically produces the clearest cross-jurisdictional analysis available in conference format.

## What We're Watching

The headline news from SuperAI 2026 will likely be a mix of partnership announcements, compute capacity disclosures, and one or two model or product reveals from the non-US AI labs in attendance. We'll cover anything builder-relevant that comes out of the keynotes — particularly if Hock (Cerebras) or Conrad (SF Compute) announce pricing or availability changes that affect the GPU access market.
