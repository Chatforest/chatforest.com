## What to Do Today

The decision framework has not changed from [Day 6](/builders-log/anthropic-fable-5-day-6-june-20-refund-deadline-builder-decision/), but the urgency has increased. The deadline is no longer "in two days" — it is tomorrow.

**If you paid for Fable 5 or Mythos 5 access during June 9–12 and have not filed for a refund:**

File today. The argument for waiting — in case an announcement comes before June 20 — still applies in theory, but the risk/reward is now heavily asymmetric. Even if an announcement comes today or tomorrow morning, the June 22 free trial window means you lose nothing by having already filed. If no announcement comes, you recover your costs. File now, through your Anthropic billing dashboard or by contacting Anthropic support.

**If you already filed or already migrated:**

Hold your current position. There is nothing to do until there is either a restoration announcement or the June 20 date passes without one.

**If you are evaluating Fable 5 for the first time:**

Wait. The June 22 free trial window — if models return — is the right entry point. Do not evaluate a suspended model under time pressure.

## The June 22 Pricing Window

A secondary deadline that has received less attention: if Fable 5 and Mythos 5 return before June 22, a free trial pricing window opens for builders who had not yet activated access. This was Anthropic's original plan for the post-launch expansion cohort.

If the models return after June 22, the free trial window is likely restructured as part of the deal terms. The specifics will depend on how restoration is announced.

Either way, June 22 is the date to watch after June 20. The refund deadline gate (June 20) closes first; the re-access gate (June 22 trial window) opens second.

## Regulatory Precedent Remains Regardless of Outcome

Worth restating once before the deadline passes: how this resolves matters for the precedent, but the precedent itself is already set.

A US government directive [suspended a commercial AI model globally three days after its June 9 public launch](https://www.forbes.com/sites/anishasircar/2026/06/16/anthropic-disabled-fable-5-and-mythos-5-after-a-u-s-export-control-order-heres-what-happened/) ([launch coverage](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/09/anthropics-claude-fable-5-is-a-version-of-mythos-the-public-can-access-today/)). That happened. The question going forward is whether it happens again, to Fable 5 or to any other frontier model from any provider. That is now a real risk category in production AI planning, separate from any capability or pricing consideration.

Builders who have already built multi-provider resilience into their stacks as a result of the June 12 incident — see the [OpenRouter resilience guide](/builders-log/openrouter-fusion-compound-ai-fable-5-alternative-builder-guide/) — are in a better position regardless of how Fable 5 returns.

## June 19 Afternoon Update: Anthropic Exec Speaks at Seoul Office Opening

**Ciauri statement ([June 18](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318668/20260618/fable-5-export-ban-day-six-anthropic-opens-seoul-office-vows-models-back-days.htm), [additional coverage](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/anthropic-confident-of-reenabling-mythos-fable-5-access-in-coming-days-executive/12727522)):** Anthropic opened its Seoul office on June 18 at Conrad Seoul. Managing Director of International **Chris Ciauri** stated directly at the press conference: *"We are very confident that in the coming days, the models will become available again."* This is the most explicit confidence statement from a named Anthropic executive since the suspension began on June 12.

The Seoul opening context matters: Anthropic is expanding internationally even as US export controls restrict access to its most capable models globally — a signal the company believes the current restriction is temporary, not structural.

**The jailbreak concern, on record:** The government's specific objection traces to [Amazon CEO Andy Jassy alerting Treasury and Commerce officials](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/13/amazon-ceo-reportedly-raised-anthropic-model-concerns-before-government-crackdown/) that [Amazon's own security researchers had found a way to prompt Fable 5 to bypass its safeguards and produce information useful for identifying software vulnerabilities](https://fortune.com/2026/06/14/how-a-warning-from-amazon-led-the-white-house-to-shut-down-anthropics-mythos-model/). That is the technical sticking point in the DoC negotiations; Anthropic has said the demonstrated jailbreak exposed only "a small number of previously known, minor vulnerabilities."

**Correction to the June 22 entry above:** The table originally described June 22 as when a "free trial window opens." That was inaccurate. June 22 is when [free plan access ends](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-fable-5-mythos-5) for Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise subscribers. Through June 22, Fable 5 (if restored) is included in those plans at no extra cost. Beginning June 23, continued use requires usage credits billed at API rates — [$10/M input tokens, $50/M output tokens](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-fable-5-mythos-5) — double the cost of [Opus 4.8](https://apidog.com/blog/claude-opus-4-8-pricing/) ($5/M input, $25/M output in standard mode). If Fable 5 returns before June 20 and you delay your refund, you would have roughly two days of free access before credits billing begins. The logic for filing by 11:59 PM June 20 remains unchanged.
