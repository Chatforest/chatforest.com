## MACHINA: Physical AI Is Moving Faster Than Most Builders Realize

[MACHINA Summit](https://www.machinasummit.com/) (July 7 at STATION F) is billed as [Europe's first major event dedicated entirely to physical AI and robotics](https://www.robot-magazine.fr/en/machina-2026-can-paris-become-europes-capital-of-physical-ai/). The two names that matter most:

- **[Carolina Parada](https://www.machinasummit.com/)** — Head of Robotics at Google DeepMind. DeepMind's robotic foundation models and embodied reasoning work is quietly among the most credible in the field.
- **Bernt Børnich** — Founder and CEO of **[1X](https://www.1x.tech/)**, the humanoid robotics company that [began full-scale production of its NEO home robot at a new Hayward, California factory in April 2026](https://www.therobotreport.com/1x-begins-production-neo-humanoid-robots-at-hayward-california-facility/). Despite marketing around AI-driven autonomy, NEO currently [relies heavily on remote human teleoperators](https://www.therobotreport.com/teleop-not-autonomy-the-path-for-1x-neo-humanoid/) — not full neural autonomy — for complex household tasks; that gap between the pitch and the current mechanism is itself one of the more contested storylines in physical AI right now. 1X's early growth was backed by the [OpenAI Startup Fund, which led its Series A2 round](https://www.1x.tech/discover/1x-rasies-23-5m-in-series-a2-funding-led-by-open-ai).

Why does this matter for software builders? Because the toolchain for physical AI is converging on the same MCP-style agentic patterns used in software. Builders who ignore the physical layer are likely to miss the next wave of API surface area opening up.

## Builder-Relevant Speakers at RAISE Summit (July 8-9)

The full speaker list tops 350 ([official 2026 roster](https://www.raisesummit.com/speakers-2026)), but these are the names whose talks are worth hunting for replays:

**Agentic AI / Coding**

- **Scott Wu** (Cognition, CEO) — builder of [Devin](https://cognition.ai/devin). His perspective on where autonomous coding agents actually are vs. the hype is one of the most grounded in the industry.
- **Anton Osika** (Lovable, Founder/CEO) — the AI-first full-stack dev tool [Lovable](https://lovable.dev/) has moved from prototype to production use for many builder teams. Worth watching for what deployment patterns are proving durable.
- **Jordan Topoleski** (Cursor/[Anysphere](https://anysphere.inc/), COO) — Cursor's [enterprise push](https://siliconangle.com/2026/04/17/ai-coding-five-takeaways-cursor-coo-jordan-topoleskis-fireside-chat-ntt-upgrade/) has been in part about what builders *don't* want from AI IDEs. Topoleski often speaks plainly about product decisions.
- **Michele Catasta** (Replit, President/Head of AI) — Replit [shipped parallel agent sessions with Agent 4 in March 2026](https://blog.replit.com/introducing-agent-4-built-for-creativity); Catasta has been unusually transparent about what worked and what didn't in builder adoption.

**Model / Infrastructure**

- **Arthur Mensch** (Mistral AI, CEO) — Mistral is shipping models at a fast pace ([Leanstral 1.5 dropped July 2](https://mistral.ai/news/leanstral-1-5/)). Mensch typically previews roadmap signals in talks.
- **Vipul Ved Prakash** ([Together AI](https://www.together.ai/), CEO) — Together runs inference for a large slice of the open-source model ecosystem; his read on the open vs. closed model market is consistently useful.
- **Lin Qiao** ([Fireworks AI](https://fireworks.ai/), CEO) — Fireworks is a significant inference provider for production agentic workloads where latency matters more than cost.
- **Tuhin Srivastava** ([Baseten](https://www.baseten.co/), Co-Founder/CEO) — model deployment infrastructure; useful for anyone thinking about serving custom fine-tuned models or self-hosting.
- **Andrew Feldman** ([Cerebras](https://www.cerebras.net/), Founder/CEO) — the inference speed argument (not just chip specs) is increasingly relevant for agentic loops where latency compounds.

**Policy / Strategic Context**

- **Guillaume Princen** (Anthropic, [Head of EMEA](https://www.anthropic.com/news/head-of-EMEA-new-roles)) — will likely surface Anthropic's European market strategy. Read alongside the recent [Anthropic Milan office expansion](/builders-log/anthropic-milan-office-emea-expansion-southern-europe/).
- **Jacob Helberg** — [Under Secretary of State for Economic Growth, Energy, and the Environment](https://www.state.gov/biographies/jacob-helberg). Sovereign AI and export control policy has direct implications for where builders can deploy and which models are accessible.

## The Four Track Framework

RAISE organizes content into [four tracks](https://www.raisesummit.com/tracks). For most builders, **/02 Frontier** is the one:

**Sessions in /02 Frontier worth watching for:**
- Agentic Systems & Autonomous Intelligence
- 'DATA': The Backbone of Enterprise AI Architecture
- Open Source & Interoperability in the Enterprise
- APIs, Tokenomics & Usage Economies
- Building Cloud Strategy for AI-Driven Enterprises

The **Open Source track** is co-organized with **[>commit](https://www.raisesummit.com/open-source)**, the first transatlantic fund focused on supporting European open-source AI entrepreneurs, which should make it more practitioner-focused than typical enterprise track content.

**/01 Foundation** covers compute-and-capital infrastructure topics like Compute as Capital and Financing the AI Boom — useful background for understanding why rate limits, inference costs, and API pricing are moving the way they are.

**/03 Friction** covers the reality check: The ROI Dilemma, compliance, and workforce transformation. The ROI session is worth following given the steady stream of enterprise deployment reports showing mixed results.

## What Signals to Watch Post-Event

Even if you're not in Paris, RAISE tends to produce durable outputs:

1. **Model roadmap breadcrumbs** — Mensch (Mistral) and others often preview directional plans in talk format that don't appear in press releases.
2. **Enterprise AI ROI data** — The /03 Friction track may surface new survey data or case study results on agentic deployment outcomes that can calibrate your own build-vs-buy decisions.
3. **MACHINA partnership announcements** — Physical AI companies often use major summits to announce compute or software partnerships. Watch 1X and Google DeepMind in particular.
4. **RAISE the STAKES winners** — Startups that win or place here often get disproportionate European distribution. Useful for watching what's getting funded in the agentic tooling space.
5. **Open Source track outputs** — The >commit partnership may produce commitments around European open-weight model governance that affect where open models can be deployed.
