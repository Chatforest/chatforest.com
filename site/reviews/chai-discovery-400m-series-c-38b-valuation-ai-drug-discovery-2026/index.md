# Chai Discovery Raises $400M Series C at $3.8B Valuation — AI Drug Discovery Reaches Deployment

> Chai Discovery closes a $400M Series C at a $3.8B valuation, tripling its price in seven months as its Chai-3 model moves from research curiosity to pharma production.


AI drug discovery startup [Chai Discovery](https://www.chaidiscovery.com/) raised [$400 million in a Series C round](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260713849009/en/Chai-Discovery-Announces-$400M-Series-C-to-Advance-AI-Driven-Molecular-Design) at a $3.8 billion valuation, [announced July 14, 2026](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/chai-discovery-announces-400m-series-130000731.html). The round triples the company's valuation from its December 2025 Series B at $1.3 billion — [announced by the company](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251214931432/en/Chai-Discovery-Announces-$130-Million-Series-B-To-Transform-Molecular-Discovery) and [confirmed by TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/15/openai-backed-biotech-firm-chai-discovery-raises-130m-series-b-at-1-3b-valuation/) — a $2.5 billion jump in roughly seven months.

## The Company

[Chai Discovery](https://www.chaidiscovery.com/) was founded in 2024 by a team that bootstrapped out of OpenAI's offices in San Francisco's Mission neighborhood before raising outside capital. [TechCrunch profiled the founding story in January 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/16/from-openais-offices-to-a-deal-with-eli-lilly-how-chai-discovery-became-one-of-the-flashiest-names-in-ai-drug-development/):

- **Joshua Meier (CEO)** — previously a senior researcher at Meta's Facebook AI Research (FAIR), where he was principal developer of the ESM-1b and ESM-1v protein language models; later [Chief AI Officer at Absci](https://www.genengnews.com/topics/artificial-intelligence/chais-the-limit-for-ai-antibody-designer-after-130m-series-b-funding/), where he led AI research including work on zero-shot antibody design.
- **Jack Dent (President)** — came from Stripe, where he [led flagship product and engineering teams](https://sapphireventures.com/blog/chai-discovery-ai-drug-discovery-series-c/).
- **Matthew McPartlon** — holds a [PhD from the University of Chicago on deep learning methods for protein design](https://knowledge.uchicago.edu/record/7659); also [worked at Absci as technical lead for de novo antibody design modeling](https://www.genengnews.com/topics/artificial-intelligence/chais-the-limit-for-ai-antibody-designer-after-130m-series-b-funding/), alongside Meier.
- **Jacques Boitreaud** — conducted [graduate research in generative molecular modeling at McGill University](https://research.contrary.com/company/chai-discovery) before leading AI at French drug-discovery firm Aqemia.

The company's core claim: AI can replace trial-and-error molecular screening with engineering-precision design, cutting pre-clinical discovery timelines from months to weeks.

## The Technology

Chai's platform predicts and reprograms interactions between biochemical molecules. Its model lineage has moved quickly:

- **Chai-2** (2025): A zero-shot generative platform for de novo antibody design, achieving a [16% experimental hit rate across 52 targets — over 100-fold higher than prior computational methods](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.07.05.663018v1), per Chai's own published preprint.
- **Chai-3** (2026): Binds therapeutic targets with materially improved affinity. Chai's own partner announcements describe it as [roughly doubling Chai-2's success rate](https://www.digitalhealthnews.com/chai-discovery-inks-agreement-with-pfizer-to-accelerate-ai-drug-discovery) — a claim [repeated in the Novartis collaboration announcement](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/chai-discovery-announces-collaboration-with-novartis-to-advance-ai-driven-antibody-discovery) — while further compressing discovery timelines.

CEO Joshua Meier [described the moment plainly](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/chai-discovery-announces-400m-series-130000731.html): "AI drug discovery has moved from promise to deployment, and Chai's models are already unlocking progress."

## Investors

[The round](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260713849009/en/Chai-Discovery-Announces-$400M-Series-C-to-Advance-AI-Driven-Molecular-Design) was led by **Index Ventures**, with **Kleiner Perkins**, **Sequoia Capital**, and **Dimension** as co-leads. New backers included Bain Capital Ventures, Battery Ventures, Baillie Gifford, BDT & MSD, [Sapphire Ventures](https://sapphireventures.com/blog/chai-discovery-ai-drug-discovery-series-c/), and Avra Capital. Returning investors include Thrive Capital, OpenAI, Oak HC/FT, Menlo Ventures, General Catalyst, Glade Brook, Avenir, Lachy Groom, and Yosemite.

The presence of OpenAI as a returning investor is notable: Chai's founders cut their teeth in OpenAI's offices, and the AI lab is now a financial stakeholder in one of the more credible applications of its own research lineage.

Total capital raised stands at approximately $630 million across four rounds — seed, a $70 million Series A, the $130 million Series B, and this $400 million Series C — [per hitconsultant.net](https://hitconsultant.net/2026/07/14/chai-discovery-raises-400-million-series-c/) and [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/14/chai-discovery-nabs-400m-series-c-ai-designed-antibodies-reach-big-pharma/), since the company was founded just two years ago.

## Pharma Partnerships

Chai has signed major collaborations with three of the largest pharmaceutical companies in the world:

- **Eli Lilly** — [announced January 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/16/from-openais-offices-to-a-deal-with-eli-lilly-how-chai-discovery-became-one-of-the-flashiest-names-in-ai-drug-development/): Lilly is using Chai's Chai-2 algorithm to accelerate biologics discovery
- **Pfizer** — [license agreement announced June 2026](https://www.digitalhealthnews.com/chai-discovery-inks-agreement-with-pfizer-to-accelerate-ai-drug-discovery): early access to Chai-3 plus a custom model trained on Pfizer's proprietary data
- **Novartis** — [collaboration announced July 2026](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/chai-discovery-announces-collaboration-with-novartis-to-advance-ai-driven-antibody-discovery): access to Chai's latest models, including Chai-3, for therapeutic antibody discovery

These deals are the key signal distinguishing Chai from the longer list of AI drug discovery companies that have raised capital but not yet touched live pharmaceutical pipelines.

## What the Valuation Arc Signals

The jump from $1.3B to $3.8B in seven months reflects two things converging: Chai-3's measurable performance improvements, and pharma's broader shift from pilot to production. When Eli Lilly, Pfizer, and Novartis are all running active Chai collaborations, the "promise" framing dissolves — these are signed partnerships with commercial-scale organizations that have their own validation processes.

The stakes in drug discovery are high: most candidates fail before ever reaching approval, and the AI shortcut through that preclinical attrition is where [Chai's platform is being applied](https://hitconsultant.net/2026/07/14/chai-discovery-raises-400-million-series-c/).

The $630 million raised so far funds a computation-intensive research program. Training and running frontier molecular models at production scale is expensive. The Series C extends Chai's runway to pursue additional partnerships and push Chai-3 capabilities further.
