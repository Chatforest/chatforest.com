Anthropic announced Claude Corps on [June 11, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-corps): a $150 million commitment to place 1,000 early-career AI practitioners at nonprofits across the United States. The program is administered by [CodePath](https://www.codepath.org/claude-corps), a [San Francisco nonprofit](https://www.codepath.org/about) focused on tech career pathways.

The window for both host organizations and fellows closes **[July 17, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-corps/host)** for the first cohort.

---

## What Claude Corps Is

Each Claude Corps Fellow receives, per [Anthropic's fellow FAQ](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-corps/fellow):
- **$85,000 annual salary** + benefits (medical, dental, vision, 401(k), paid holidays and flexible PTO)
- **12 months full-time placement** inside a host nonprofit
- **5 hours per week** of ongoing Claude training and professional development from Anthropic
- A Claude token budget for building during the placement, plus up to $2,500 in Claude API credits for approved host projects

Host organizations receive, per [Anthropic's host FAQ](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-corps/host):
- **Fully covered salary and benefits** for each fellow (no cost to the org)
- **$10,000 one-time implementation grant** — paid to the host organization, not the fellow, administered through Social Finance to cover operational/admin costs
- **Up to $2,500 in Claude licenses and API credits** per fellow

Cohort 1 starts October 19, 2026. There are three planned cohorts: October 2026 (100 fellows), January 2027, and August 2027 — [roughly 100, 450, and 450 fellows respectively](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-corps/fellow).

---

## The Gating Requirement Builders Need to Know

For Cohort 1, host organizations must already be **[Claude for Nonprofits Team or Enterprise customers](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-corps/host)**. Fully remote organizations [can apply now but won't be eligible to host until the 2027 cohorts](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-corps/host), not October 2026 — hosts must have an in-person or hybrid office where the fellow can spend regular time with the team.

This is a meaningful filter. It means the first cohort goes to organizations that are already paying Anthropic subscribers — not orgs evaluating Claude for the first time. If your nonprofit is not yet an Anthropic customer and you want to host a Cohort 1 fellow, you need to become one before July 17.

Anthropic has confirmed the remote-work exclusion lifts for the 2027 cohorts. It has not said whether the Anthropic-customer requirement itself will loosen for Cohorts 2 and 3 — that part is this article's own expectation, not a stated Anthropic commitment.

## Who Can Apply as a Fellow

Fellows must, per [Anthropic's fellow FAQ](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-corps/fellow):
- Be **over 18** and authorized to work in the United States (no visa sponsorship available)
- Have **fewer than 2 years of full-time work experience** (any field, any background — no degree required)
- Be comfortable working with Claude and already use AI tools daily
- Be willing to **relocate if necessary** for an in-person or hybrid placement, though relocation support is available

The target is early-career workers, with priority for candidates committed to a cause or community. CodePath, which administers placement, is a large operator in this space: it has [trained more than 40,000 students from 1,000 colleges who now work at 4,000 companies](https://www.codepath.org/claude-corps), with roughly 70% of its students coming from low-income backgrounds.

Applications for Cohort 1 (100 fellows) close **July 17, 2026** and begin October 19. [Subsequent cohorts expand to the full 1,000 over 2026–2027](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-corps/fellow).

## What Fellows Actually Do

Fellows are embedded full-time inside the nonprofit to implement practical AI workflows using Claude. Announced host organizations named on [Anthropic's own launch post](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-corps) include RAINN, Goodwill Industries International, Code for America, Year Up United, YMCA of Greater Charlotte, and Montgomery County Food Bank (also reported by [Quartz](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/anthropic-launches-150m-claude-corps-170834614.html)), alongside Braven, Code the Dream, Heartland Forward, Team Red White & Blue, Reef Environmental Education Foundation, SoundOff, StriveTogether, and the International Rescue Committee.

The work is not AI research or model development — it is applied deployment. Anthropic's launch post describes fellow projects ranging from [data analysis for StriveTogether to automating organizational management for SoundOff and streamlining operations into AI-powered services for the International Rescue Committee](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-corps).

Host organizations must provide a **[sponsor](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-corps/host)** (a senior leader who champions the fellowship internally) and a **supervisor** (a staff member who manages the fellow day-to-day). Each org can host up to four fellows, though Anthropic recommends at least two when feasible.

## What This Signals for the Claude Ecosystem

Claude Corps is not primarily a PR initiative. There are three ecosystem effects worth tracking:

**1. Enterprise customer seeding.** The requirement that hosts be Anthropic nonprofit Enterprise or Team customers accelerates Claude's footprint in the mission-driven sector. Every fellow placement locks in at least one year of active Claude usage at an organization that may not have prioritized it otherwise.

**2. A trained practitioner cohort.** A thousand Claude-trained practitioners, placed across [400+ organizations](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-corps), will enter the broader job market starting in late 2027 (12 months after the last cohort's October/January/August 2026–2027 start dates). They will carry deployment patterns, workflow templates, and model preferences established during their fellowship. In the same way that early AWS training programs shaped cloud adoption in the 2010s, Claude Corps graduates will shape AI adoption at nonprofits and adjacent sectors.

**3. Benchmark for what Claude can do without developers.** Most Claude deployment today requires a builder to integrate APIs, write prompts, and wire up tools. Claude Corps is testing whether non-developer practitioners can run effective Claude workflows directly. If they can, the total addressable deployment surface for Claude expands well beyond teams with engineering resources.
